1.6.0 Beta 4

November 12, 2025

This is a work-in-progress beta version. There may be incomplete features or bugs, and everything is subject to change. Make backups, proceed at your own risk, and have fun!

This version includes the final set of additions and changes for the 1.6 update. If no further bugs or changes needed to be made, this will be the 1.6 release.

Additions

Miscellaneous Additions

All new features now have proper compendium entries

Changes

Root Dye Recipes

Hanging roots are now included with the nether roots in dye smelting.

They can now be smelted into brown dye, giving it a secondary source.

Frostbloom

Tripled the frostbloom ice rates

  • This is a temporary balancing hotfix
  • There will be a better fix in the future to improve ice farms

Creaking Crypt

  • Loot tables have now been finalized with proper loot
  • Added two new rooms to the basement
  • Increased the chances of eyeblossoms generating on the basement floor
  • Fixed terrain adaptation being wonky sometimes

Removals

Compass Cloning Recipe

Removed this feature I added in 1.6 Beta 1.

This felt weird since it effectively meant you didn’t have to spend any dye to dye your compasses once you’ve dyed one. The output also depended on where in the crafting grid you placed the compasses, which was also weird.
