0.3.0: Gold and Gems

June 23, 2020

This release of TCC has been a very fun one in that it has had LOTS of content shoved into one update. I never expected that there would be a nether update coming, and the nether reactor and cursed crown for TCC came along with it. Not only was there lots of content, but there were lots of devs working on it as well. In the last month of TCC dev, we acquired a new dev– ChromaKey! He’s created a couple small new features, and will be creating much more in the days to come. Not only did Chroma join the team, but dragoncommands has also proved his worth by reworking lots of content and becoming somewhat of an idea powerhouse on the team.

Anyways, all the talk about the development process aside, here’s the meat of this post… The changelog! This update was themed around Gold and Gems, (and also Glowing), and there’s plenty for each of those categories to follow.

Additions

Cursed Crown

A rare head decoration you can find in bastions. When worn on your head, it turns the drops of all the mobs you kill into golden nuggets. Yikes. Or maybe not, depending on if you want gold.

Enchanted Golden Carrot

These are crafted like golden carrots except with ingots instead of nuggets. They give you night vision for five minutes.

Enchanted Golden Apple Recipe

Readded the removed recipe from 1.9. I don’t like having certain items that are required for advancements being loot-exclusive.

Nether Reactor

This isn’t the old nether reactor from the days of bedrock, as it has a completely different function. This nether reactor, made from blackstone, gilded blackstone, and netherite, gives all friendly mobs in a radius fire resistance, clears wither I from players, and gives striders speed III. The specifics can be found on its feature page.

Player Head Recipe

You can now craft your own head with a glistering melon and a skeleton skull.

Absorption Improvements

Golden apples and Enchanted golden apples now can stack absorption, meaning if you eat a golden apple while you have two absorption hearts, it’ll add two on to give you four! The maximum amount for this is 10 hearts.

Totem Improvements

Totems will now save you from the void if you fall by giving you levitation and slow falling, and they will also give you water breathing if you drown, and fire resistance if you burn in lava.

Rings

Masons will now trade gemstones

Toolsmiths will now trade iron rings with gems

Added gemstones, which can be made by dropping a crystalline item (Diamond, Emerald, Ender Pearl, Lapis, Obsidian, Prismarine Crystal, Quartz) onto a stonecutter and waiting a little bit.

  • Each gemstone gives a specific attribute, which are listed on their respective feature pages.

Jewelry Table

Added the jewelry table, which allows you to apply potion effects, enchantments like flame, thorns, and channeling, and gemstones to rings.

Unbreaking, mending, fire aspect, thorns, and channeling can now be applied to rings via jewelry table.

Potion effects can now be applied to rings via jewelry table. Don’t worry though, you can still apply them via lingering effect cloud, so your wither swords are safe. 😉

Spectral Boomerang

A glowing variant of the boomerang that can go through walls. When it hits mobs, they glow and take a bit of damage.

Anvil Smashing

Anvils can now smash glass and glowstone. When glowstone is smashed, it gives lots of ‘fun’ potion effects to the surrounding mobs.

Emoji

Added new villager emoji activated by typing /trigger tcc.emoji. There’s lots of fun emoji you can use, most of which have already been installed on the discord server. Go check them out!

Accessible Music Discs

Creepers killed by piglins will now drop pigstep. Great for those of you without any bastions nearby!

Charged Music Discs

Charged music discs, dropped by charged creepers killed by skeletons (or piglins.) When played in a jukebox, they will change the weather to thunder as long as the song lasts.

Player Hoppers

This was a very fun feature to test. Basically, players can now jump in a hopper and be transported down the chain, unless it’s blocked or too long.

Harvester

A new hoe block breaker variant

Wrench

Wrenches can now see how many bees are in a hive if you shift-click a nest wih it

Frost Walker Mounts

Frost walker now works when riding horses

Climbable Chains

Chains are now climbable

Fortune Shovel Sifting

Shovels with fortune will now get you more loot when mining out sandy blocks, like podzol, soul sand, and mycelium

Silk Touch Improvements

Silk touch can now pick up more blocks, like filled cauldrons, inverted daylight sensors, charged respawn anchors, grass paths, farmland, etc.

Miscellaneous Additions

A handy-dandy crafting menu located in the advancements tab. There’s also a fun toast that pops up whenever you unlock a new custom recipe.

Added a bunch more fun advancements for you get while you’re playing the pack

More bugs

Changes

Boomerang

Boomerangs now bounce off walls and come back to you no matter what

Boomerangs now do much less damage, but still ignore armor. 1 heart, rather than 3, so they’re not insanely overpowered anymore.

Obsidian Scythe

Obsidian scythes can now mine out large areas of crops and replant them all in one go

Elytra Closing

Elytra now show a actionbar message when you close them midair, so you know when you’re starting to plummet to your death

Ancient Acorn

Ancient Acorns now give Haste II instead of health boost, since health boost would’ve broken a lot of my code

Compendium

It’s all been reworked. It’s now a whole lot better. Recipes, indexes, feature versions, wiki links, the works. It’s a beautiful sight to behold.

Quest Books

Quest books have now been changed so they give notifications in the top left when you complete an item. Their difficulty has also been tweaked.

Miscellaneous Changes

Got rid of some uneccessary files that replaced vanilla content

Generally improved TCC code, fixing bugs and such

Removals

Charms

All the functionality of the charm system has either been shifted into gemstones/other features, or completely removed.

Potion Potatoes

This was for the best. Potion potatoes added nothing existing potions didn’t, and were just inventory clutter. Plus, the code was really messy.

Rotten Porkchop

Eating rotten porkchops no longer angers zombie pigm… err.. sorry… zombified piglins. First, 1.16 broke this feature, and second, yay! Now you can munch down on that rotten pork in peace!

Chorus Beetle

Chorus Beetles. Say goodbye to the chorus beetle for this update, but not for too long. Next update, he’ll come back in full force with a whole new look and new behavior. I just need a little more time to fix him up… 1.16 broke a lot of his behavior.

Editor’s note from the future: He did not, in fact, return since he was super lame.

Wool to String Recipe

Why doesn’t this work for dyed wool? How are you just pulling the wool apart into threads? Doesn’t make sense.

More Arrow Recipes

Iron nuggets and obsidian shards giving more arrows than flint: This just didn’t make sense at all. You use the same amount of sticks, why should you get more arrows?

Grass Block Recipe

Pressing grass onto dirt isn’t gonna make it grow

Increased Sandstone Recipe Yield

4 sandstone from 4 sand: Normal 4:1 ratio makes more sense, you’re compressing sand into sandstone

Faster Kelp Smelting

Didn’t make sense, since if kelp cooked faster, then kelp blocks shouldn’t last longer as fuel. Also was overpowered for dried kelp fuel farms.

Rotten Flesh to Leather Smoking

This was a little bit overpowered. Besides, bat wings are already early-game leather.
