0.2.0: Debugging and Treehugging

June 9, 2019

This update fixes bugs introduced to TCC from 1.14, and reworks a lot of features. (The Debugging part!)

It also adds a bunch of nature-related features. (The Treehugging part!)

Overall, I believe this is one of the best TCC updates I’ve ever made, considering it wasn’t made by just me! I was helped with some of the features in this update by dragoncommands, a new developer for the datapack. He didn’t do much other than stand around and act as a test dummy for this update, but for the next update, he’s going to contribute quite a bit more.

(He better, otherwise he doesn’t get fed.)

Additions

Ancient Acorn

A new food item dropped rarely from spruce trees. When eaten, it gives health boost for a long amount of time.

Apple Pies

New food items crafted using two apples, one egg, and one sugar. You can eat a slice out of them 3 times, each time getting smaller. Golden apple pie has the same effects as golden apples.

Biome Based Fishing

Added biome-based fishing loot (With new rewards!)

You now fish up tropical fish more in warm oceans, swordfish up more in cold oceans, etc.

You can also fish up shulker shells instead of nautilus shells in the end.

Levitation Suspicious Stew

Crafted with chorus flowers, gives levitation for a short period of time

Sweet Berry Muffin

A new food item made from sweet berries

Silk Touch Improvements

Grass paths and farmland can be silk-touched

Bat Wing

A new item dropped by bats that can craft into leather

Golden Rings

Golden Rings (with charms and potion-soaking)

The potions have slightly shorter durations but longer durabilities. Or maybe it’s the other way around? I forget.

Wrench Recipe

Wrenches can now be crafted with iron

Editor’s note from the future: I genuinely have no idea how you got them previously. These old changelogs are missing a LOT of detail.

Trident Repair

Tridents can be repaired in a crafting table

Charm Additions

A new charm and hex animation

The Charm of Sifting now finds different items in different biomes, and the loot has been generally updated.

New charms/hexes:

  • The Charm of Momentum, a golden ring charm which gives you varying levels of speed, depending on the amount of time you’ve been sprinting. The more you run, the faster you go!
  • The Charm of Attraction, a golden ring charm which attracts any nearby items to you, if you didn’t drop them on purpose and your inventory isn’t full.
  • The Hex of Rotting, a new obsidian scythe hex which causes potato crops that you mine to have a higher chance of being poisonous

All horse armor charms are now saddle charms, so you can put them on skeleton horses, mules, and donkeys! Also, the charm of frost trotting actually works now.

Elytra Closing

You can now close your elytra mid-air to go into freefall by shifting. This allows for some cool tricks.

Snipper

A new kind of block breaker which breaks shears-related blocks.

Potion Potatoes

Only potatoes can be soaked in potion effects, instead of all foods, to make the feature less overpowered. However, potion-soaked potatoes can also be found in potato crops rarely.

Compendium

A handy-dandy encyclopedia for all things TCC! Crafted with a piece of gunpowder and a book.

Edible Swordfish

Swordfish can now be eaten, but beware, they are swords after all!

Miscellaneous Additions

All items with durability can now have unbreaking, mending, and the curse of vanishing

Updated potion-tipped/soaked items (Maintain names, will now run out)

Some new advancements

Changes

Block Breakers

Block Breakers will now get the block they break within their inventory, instead of dropping it out on the ground. They will also not pick up blocks if their inventory is full, and will also ‘fart’ out blocks in their inventory when powered. 😛

Obsidian Shard

Obsidian Shards can now only be made via blast furnace

Rings

Shields now require iron rings to craft

Cracked Nether Star

The cracked nether star is now created by dropping an anvil on a nether star

The cracked nether star now gives one random beacon effect for 5 minutes, and multiple cannot be applied at once

Rotten Flesh to Leather Smoking

Rotten flesh can now only be turned into leather in a smoker.

Obsidian Mirror Rework

The obsidian mirror has received a rework, which changes it into a handheld item which flips potion effects, instead of a wall-mounted mirror which puts you into spectator mode.

Hex Changes

The ‘Hex of Fatigue’ has been renamed to the ‘Hex of Sloth’. Sloth just sounded better, and gets rid of any association with mining fatigue.

The Hex of Midas Touch has been edited so that now it will give you bad luck, which makes mobs from drop only gold nuggets when you use the sword. (King Midas couldn’t touch anything, it all turned to gold. So now, neither can you!)

Obsidian Scythe

The obsidian scythe is now more of a farming tool: Crops harvested with it will now drop extra loot.

Peculiar Berries

Peculiar Berries are now traded by the wandering trader instead of being found randomly in grass.

Miscellaneous Changes

TCC advancements and all items now show ‘The Creeper’s Code’ in their description.

Removals

Potion Soaked Foods

Removed as this was overpowered. Only potatoes can be soaked in potions now, see above.

Enchanted Items

Removed the following enchanted loot items:

  • Enchanted Rotten Flesh: Was useless, and broke in 1.14.
  • Enchanted Ghast Tear: Was useless, and broke in 1.14.
  • Enchanted mushroom stew: See the other enchanted items. You get the idea.

Lumberjack

Removed lumberjacks. They got broken in 1.14 ;-;

Charm Removals

Removed the charms of Sturdiness and Everlasting. Sturdiness was removed as it clashed with custom durabilities, while Everlasting was removed to due unfixable bugs.

Chorus Beetle Horn

Removed the Chorus Beetle Horn. The grindstone pretty much broke it. Don’t worry though, the chorus beetle will get another drop in the future.
