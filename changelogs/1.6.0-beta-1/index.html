1.6.0 Beta 1

September 12, 2025

This is a work-in-progress beta version. There may be incomplete features or bugs, and everything is subject to change. Make backups, proceed at your own risk, and have fun!

It’s finally time for public betas again!

This time around, I’m adding to the mansion and pale garden with new content and general improvements.

This post intentionally is lacking images so you experience these structures for the first time in-game, rather than being swayed by a few screenshots.

Have fun, and as always, make sure to test and report bugs on our discord!

Additions

Creaking Crypt

  • A new archaeology structure found in pale gardens
  • It is not complete, there will be an additional basement section added later
  • Includes a new armor trim, new banner pattern, and new resin pottery

Resin Brick Pottery

  • There are 8 new resin pottery sherds:
    • Awaken (Open eyeblossom)
    • Blight (Pale oak tree)
    • Link (Saplink)
    • Moon (Crescent moon)
    • Shade (Dark oak tree)
    • Slumber (Closed eyeblossom)
    • Stare (Eye)
    • Watcher (Creaking eyes)
  • Awaken, Blight, Link, Stare, and Watcher can be found in creaking crypts.
  • Moon, Shade, and Slumber can be found in woodland mansion basements
  • There’s also a resin brick flower pot to go with this

Crescent Banner Pattern

  • A new banner pattern found in creaking crypts
  • Puts a moon pattern on your banner when used

Resin Candy

  • A new food item crafted with resin clumps and sugar
  • Gives more saturation when eaten at night
  • Perfect for silly Halloween-styled builds

Learning Enchantment

  • This enchantment is mutually exclusive with looting
  • It increases XP drops from mobs

Ancient City Loot Rework

  • Ancient city loot has been reworked to include less clutter
  • There are now only 2 discs: “5” and “ward”, rather than 4 discs
  • Learning books, mending books, enchanted golden carrots, and soul lanterns have been added to the loot pool
  • Saddles, soul torches, regeneration potions, and leads have been removed from the loot pool

Brush Painting

When holding a brush and a dye in opposite hands, you can now brush the dye onto dyeable blocks.

This process does not use up any dye, instead using brush durability

  • This works with unbreaking, meaning an unbreaking III brush can paint quite a bit

Root Dye Recipes

Cyan dye can now be made by smelting down nether sprouts

Increased Tnt Yield

Quadrupled the yield of the TNT recipe

Craftable Mushroom Blocks

New recipes for mushroom blocks using a 2x2 of mushrooms

Compass Cloning Recipe

Compasses can now be cloned by placing two compasses in the crafting grid

Changes

More Flower Pots

Added a new resin brick flower pot to go with resin brick pottery

Woodland Mansion Rework

  • The mansion has been completely reworked once again
  • The gameplay is mostly the same, but much of the build has been updated
  • This includes:
    • Consistent bookshelf, chandelier, shelf, and table style
    • More room variations & removal of boring rooms
    • Bringing back better versions of missing vanilla rooms
    • An overhaul of the exterior to be more in line with vanilla
  • There are also 3 new hidden rooms accessible via chiseled bookshelf
  • Archaeology is now guaranteed in the basement section, and it includes a map to the creaking crypt

Ancient City Archaeology

Since the woodland mansion now has resin brick pottery, the sherds found there have instead been moved to the ancient city, resulting in a few new unique archaeology sites.

Armor Trim Addon

Added a new armor trim to the armor trim addon: Dusk, exclusively found in creaking crypts.

Also touched up some textures on the other armor trims.

Removals

Paintbrush

The title of this one’s a bit misleading. The painting functionality of the paintbrush isn’t removed, I removed it and replaced it with vanilla items. See brush painting.

The only downside of this is that the painting cycling feature has been removed

  • However, don’t worry, we’re still looking into alternatives to make your painting experience less annoying.
