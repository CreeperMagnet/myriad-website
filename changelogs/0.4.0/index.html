0.4.0: End and Emeralds

February 17, 2021

This update has been a rollercoaster ride of an experience. I’ve reworked a massive majority of the code, added amazing new content, and made daily developer updates all the way through. However, I didn’t ride this rollercoaster alone.

Along the way, I picked up three (THREE!!!) new developers, who have all helped in their own ways with this update. Nexus and Jachro joined early on in the process, giving feedback and helping out quite a bit. Ellivers joined late in the development cycle, helped me rework tons and tons of code, created some awesome art and sounds, and has become extremely comfortable with our codebase. It’s honestly pretty impressive.

Not only did I pick up new developers, but the developers I already had seriously upped their skills. Rag gave me tons upon tons of new ideas, Chroma added even more code, and Dragon was lazy. Yeah, that last one doesn’t quite fit, but it’s true.

Anyways, essays about the dev process aside, let’s get into what you guys really want— the trailer and changelog!

This update is called “The End and Emerald Update”, with ‘End’ features such as the positional anchor, tasty tea, massive expansions to the end cities, and a mysterious carnivorous plant called the gobblerift… For the ‘Emerald’ side, we’ve got several emerald-loving illagers on the plate, from the Iceologer to the Enchanter, as well as the structures that both of them live in.

Alright, let’s get into the massive wall of text!

Additions

Positional Anchor

A useful redstone block to keep pearls in stasis. Put an ender pearl in it, and power it with redstone to launch the pearl later.

Warped Wart

A variant of nether wart crafted with warped sprouts and nether wart and used in tea. Warped wart blocks are also now craftable using 2x the warped wart recipe.

Tea

Tea and teapots. Place a teapot atop a campfire, pour in a bottle of water, and add some warped wart. Add the right ingredient to make a tea.

  • Each tea grants immunity to a status effect. Only one immunity is applied at a time; drinking a different tea overrides the current one.
  • Wither Rose -> Tea of Purity, Wither immunity
  • Honey Bottle -> Tea of Antitoxin, Poison immunity
  • Glowstone Dust -> Tea of Clearsight, Blindness immunity
  • Sweet Berries -> Tea of Plenty, Hunger immunity
  • Blaze Powder -> Tea of Vigor, Weakness immunity
  • Rabbit’s Foot -> Tea of Serendipity, Bad Luck immunity
  • Ink Sac -> Tea of Obscurity, Glowing immunity
  • Dandelion -> Tea of Steadiness, Nausea immunity
  • Ancient Acorn -> Tea of Vitality, Mining Fatigue immunity
  • Riftjuice -> Tea of Stability, Levitation immunity (See ‘Gobblerift’)
  • Frostbloom Petals -> Tea of Vivacity, Slowness immunity (See “Frostbloom Petals”)

Gobblerift

A mysterious carnivorous plant found within end city additions. If grown to full size and fed plenty of meat, it’ll produce riftjuice, which teleports you back to the gobblerift when bottled and consumed.

Riftjuice Bottle

Produced from gobblerifts, teleports you back to the gobblerift when you drink it.

End City Additions

Small extensions of the end city found about 66% of the time. Contains gobblerifts and plenty of danger in the form of shulkers.

Golden Chorus Fruit

A golden version of the normal chorus fruit that teleports you much further.

Village and Pillage Map

A special map always sold by the Wandering Trader. They have a 50/50 chance of either leading to a pillager outpost (or outpost variant) or a village.

Chilling Banner

A new ominous banner variant that spawns in tundra keeps

Tundra Keep

A pillager outpost variant that generates in snowy biomes. Iceologers and pillagers occupy the Keep, who protect a room containing a Frostbloom.

Iceologer

The rejected illager from the mob vote. Drops ice onto players’ heads. Don’t even think about trying to hide underneath a ceiling; the iceologer is smarter than that and will freeze you if they notice. They spawn in the Tundra Keep and in raids within cold biomes.

Frostbloom

A magic flower that produces snow around its position and freezes water to ice, ice to packed ice, and packed ice to blue ice. It can be found in the Tundra Keep and rarely dropped by iceologers (except iceologers spawning in raids).

Frostbloom Petals

Dropped occasionally by strays and iceologers. When eaten, they give a short regeneration effect, and a longer frozen screen overlay and slowness effect. They are also used to brew the tea of vivacity. (See ‘Tea’)

Woodland Mansion Basement

A deadly area found beneath the woodland mansion. It’s filled with illagers such as the enchanter, and has lots of good loot, including the Locket of Learning.

Enchanter

A direct port from Minecraft Dungeons. It gives nearby illagers and zombies positive effects, and drops the locket of learning and plenty of other nice loot.

Locket of Learning

A useful magic tool used to store experience. It can hold up to 30 levels.

Witch Trading

A risky way to get some useful goods by wearing a witch hat and trading a witch some spider eyes or potion ingredients.

Pristine Gemstone Overlay

A new overlay for pristine gemstones

Tipped Swords

Added back the lore on wither-tipped swords dropped by wither skeletons, something that was accidentally removed last update.

Campfire Improvements

Campfires now drop unlit campfires instead of raw materials when broken with a non-Silk Touch axe

Pig Improvements

Pigs now always breed two children, with a 50% chance of breeding a third

Village Beehives

Occupied beehives now generate on some village farms

Anvil Repairing

Anvils can now be repaired fully in a smithing table using a block of iron

Cake Improvements

Cake no longer drops nothing when broken, and now drops the proper amount of slices

Elder Guardian Improvements

Killing elder guardians now clears any mining fatigue you may have

Jeb Sheep Drops

Sheep named “jeb_” will now drop a random color of wool on death

Frost Walker Speed

The frost walker enchantment will now give you a speed boost when on ice

Wrench

Wrenches can now take the stats of mules and donkeys too, rather than just horses

Wrenches can now rotate a whole lot more blocks

Mob Equippable Headwear

Witch hats and cursed crowns can now be put on armor stands and other mobs

Jungle Temple Changes

The arrows in the jungle temple are now tipped with poison

Miscellaneous Additions

Way too many splashes. Seriously, the sheer number is insane.

A whole lot of new advancements.

Bunches of new sounds for old and new content alike. Thanks, Elli!

Changes

Jewelry Table

Jewelry tables can now repair gemstone rings simply by adding a gemstone

Rings

Iron and golden rings are now unstackable

  • The ring code has also been heavily tweaked, allowing easy implementation of third-party rings and gemstones.

Balanced the bonuses given by quartz and prismarine crystal rings from 15/30% to 10/20%

Smoldering Seeker

Smoldering seekers can now be found in ruined portal loot

Peculiar Berries

The peculiar berry particles are now purple instead of enchanting particles

Absorption Improvements

Tweaked some values for the absorption stacking feature

Lingering Grace Potion

Grace potions can now be applied to rings and swords

Boomerang

Allowed boomerangs to damage withers

Block Breakers

Block breakers now support the efficiency enchantment, which allows them to break multiple blocks in front of them

Block breakers now respect the DoTileDrops gamerule

The ‘fortune shovel sifting feature now applies to block breakers (See bug #11)

Anvil Smashing

Anvils can now crack blocks they’re dropped onto

The duration of the effects given by smashing glowstone with an anvil has been heavily reduced

Anvils now have to drop 3+ blocks in order to crack or smash blocks

Witch Hat

Tripled the rate at which witch hats drop

The witch hat now uses the witch texture, meaning it has resource pack compatibility

Cursed Crown

Improved drop rates for cursed crowns from soul sand

Fortune Shovel Sifting

Nerfed fortune shovel loot from soul soil and soul sand

Rotten Porkchop

Rotten porkchops anger zombified piglins once again

Obsidian Scythe

Tweaked how the obsidian scythe lore appears

Chainmail Armor Recipes

Chainmail armor is now crafted with the new chains instead of iron rings

Miscellaneous Changes

Changed the color of the “The Creeper’s Code” tooltip to a nice shade of green

Added a bunch more consistency for creative mode interactions

Boomerangs and other custom damage sources now take resistance and armor into account

Changed a whole lot of textures (Thanks, Ninni!)

And, finally, heavily optimized the pack. I’ve also restructured the entirety of the code.

Removals

End Crystal Dragon Head Healing

Removed the extremely laggy pointer from end crystal to player when wearing a dragon head

Spyglass

Removed since a basic carbon copy of it is being added in 1.17.

Player Hoppers

Poppers, short for player hoppers. To put it simply, this feature was dumb and it sucked.

Cracked Nether Star

It was stupid, overpowered, and had a stupid way of making it.
