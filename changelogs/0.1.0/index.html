0.1.0: A Clean Slate

October 23, 2018

So, how long has this taken? Too long, that’s how long. I’m releasing V0.1 for now, only because I need to release something before the 1.14 snapshots! I’m cutting it a bit close, considering the first snapshot is tomorrow. The 1.14 snapshots are gonna screw things up in a good way, and the development will start again! All my features are complete right now, and you should be able to expect an optimization update soon. (V0.1.1, I guess. Thanks, my crappy naming system.) Basically, things are laggy at the moment, and I just want to get a version released. So if you experience lag, please fix it yourself because I won’t have the time to bother! <3

Expect updates to come out more quickly after this, as 1.13 was a whole ton slower than 1.14 should be. (I hope!)

Everything from past versions has been wiped away, and recreated with a better codebase. I’m now building off of a clean slate of sorts.

Additions

Charms

Charmers are villagers which will ‘charm’ your items for emeralds. Charms are basically custom enchantments, only obtainable from charmers or chest loot. They can also hex your items, so watch out.

Several new charms have been added, to both new TCC tools, and vanilla tools. Previous custom enchantments have been ported over to the new charm system. Some new charms are: Everlasting, which can be applied to milk or water buckets to make them never run out, along with several horse armor charms.

Boomerang

A new weapon made from leather and wood. Right click to throw, and if it doesn’t hit a block, it will come back after it has traveled 50 blocks. It can be charmed with charms or hexes that can allow it to bounce off walls, track you, or even work underwater.

Obsidian Shard

Created from smelting obsidian in a furnace. Takes a while, but can be crafted into obsidian scythes, arrows, and mirrors.

Obsidian Scythe

A new tool and weapon, which has a very heavy attack, long cooldown, and a sweep attack. Imagine a sword mixed with an axe. They can be charmed to steal health from what you hit, or to ‘vein-mine’ fully grown crops.

Obsidian Mirror

A new tool, which if you gaze into it while it is in an item frame, it puts you into gamemode spectator for 20-ish seconds, and then teleports you back to the now broken mirror afterwards. Crafted with an ender eye and 8 obsidian shards.

More Arrow Recipes

Tripwire hooks are now made from iron rings, instead of iron ingots.

Iron Rings

A new item which replaces all old ring variants, which can be both charmed, and tipped with potion effects. Potion-soaked rings will give you a short duration of their effect on right-click in the offhand. Charmed rings will give you small buffs, or debuffs if they are hexed. Iron rings are crafted from 8 iron nuggets in a ring formation, and can be used to craft chain armor, tripwire hooks, and saddles. They can also be fished up either charmed or not.

Swordfish

A new weapon which can be fished up as a treasure. It does about as much damage as a stone sword, but since it is a fish, it can’t be enchanted and has no durability. It also cannot be tipped with potions.

Wool to String Recipe

Added a new recipe to craft white wool into 4 string

Increased Sandstone Recipe Yield

The sandstone recipe now crafts 4 sandstone from 4 sand instead of 1

Improved Prismarine Recipes

All prismarine recipes now line up better with other recipes in the game.

Everything is derived from prismarine blocks instead of being a weird combination of shards

More Stone Substitutions

Stone tools can now be crafted from all variants of stone

Horse Armor Improvements

Horse armors are now craftable

Chorus Beetle Horn

Added the chorus beetle horn, a new weapon which does immense knockback, gives crazy amounts of speed, and is dropped by chorus beetles. Its durability of 64 uses can be used either by hitting mobs, or by right clicking.

Rogue Quest

Added a new ‘rogue’ quest. (Idea from Jish!)

End Crystal Dragon Head Healing

End crystals will now both heal and point to players with dragon heads on

Drowned Loot Changes

Drowned will drop prismarine crystals instead of gold ingots. (Where’d they even get those?)

They also drop tridents more often if they are holding them.

Peculiar Berries

Enchanted seeds have been replaced by peculiar berries, which can be found by either mining tall grass or finding them in dungeon chests

Consistent Stair Recipe Yield

All stair recipes now output 8 stairs instead of 4

Coral Recipes

You can now craft coral blocks using a 2x2 of coral or coral fans. This also works with the dead variants.

Faster Kelp Smelting

Kelp now smelts into dried kelp faster in a furnace.

Miscellaneous Additions

Items from TCC will now have a tooltip in their lore that says “The Creeper’s Code”.

A few new advancements

Changes

Block Breakers

Block breakers now rely on block tags, which are easily editable using datapacks of your own!

Wrench

Wrenches now use raycasting and can rotate more things. (They can even open unpowered iron trapdoors!)

Miscellaneous Changes

Lang files now use proper vanilla-like structure

Updated splash texts

Updated the initiation functions: TCC will now tell you if you don’t have the resource pack or if you’re playing on the wrong version

Updated the entire pack to 1.13. Probably the biggest change this version.

Removals

Old Rings

Removed the old ring system, replaced with the new iron rings.

Insane Dark Times Features

Editor’s note from the future: For the sake of my sanity, features predating 0.1.0 have not been included in the wiki or this changelog. Below is the text from the 0.1.0 changelog, not integrated into the wiki backend. Yes, a lot of these things existed. Yes, they all sound totally ridiculous.

  • Removed the chisel and stonemason villager, chisel’s blocks were removed with ‘the flattening’
  • Removed quite a few custom advancements, as they didn’t really add anything, and were annoying.
  • Removed obsidian swords, as they have been replaced with obsidian scythes
  • Removed ships in bottles, as Mojang added shipwrecks. (They simply don’t work together!) But don’t worry, spyglasses are still a thing, and they’re found in shipwrecks.
  • Redid some loot:
    • Removed loot books, as they messed with progression in a bad way.
    • Removed the sponge drop from normal guardians, and added it to drowned instead
    • Iron golems no longer drop increased amounts of iron
    • Removed the luck stat, except for the misfortune charm on iron rings, used to get cracked nether stars
    • Removed the lucky rabbit’s foot, as luck is gone now
    • Witches no longer drop potions they don’t drink
    • Witches no longer drop enchanted sticks, as they’re brewers, not enchanters
    • Silverfish no longer drop iron nuggets, as that made no sense
    • Strays no longer drop packed ice, as Mojang added a packed ice recipe
    • Chorus beetles no longer drop elytra, as that was a tad bit overpowered. Now, their rare drop has been replaced with the chorus beetle horn.

Removed for now:

  • Runes. They’re getting a massive idea rework, and it’ll be a while before they pop up again.
  • Arcane masks. They probably won’t be returning, but I can’t know for sure. Them and runes are entangled a bit, and may end up being combined.
  • Totems making items unbreakable. I’m looking into a way to make this work, but I don’t want to have to drop items on the ground. (anvils?)
  • Death points, waypoints, sleeping messages, and whereami triggers. These are actually getting removed from TCC, as they’re becoming part of a new project, which is going to be my main focus for a while.
  • The chisel. The blocks that it used to create the ‘chiseled’ variants have been removed as of 1.13, which is very sad. 🙁
