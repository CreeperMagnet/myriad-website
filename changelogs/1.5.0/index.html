1.5.0: Pots and Plants and Potions

January 31, 2025

Yeah, it’s been a while since the last update. A whole new drop cycle has started since 1.4.1 came out and you all have been pleading for a 1.21.4 port.

Well, here it is finally—we got a little caught up in adding fun features with new 1.21.4 tech and muddied up our porting and development.

This update may seem a bit smaller than usual for a major release, but I can tell you that the effort put into it is well worth it. Lots of tech changes went on behind the scenes, even if there aren’t many new features.

Most of the features we have just happened to fall into three nice categories—”pots”, for all the new pot blocks, “plants”, for the gobblerift rework and creaking connector, and “potions” for dyeable witch hats and the new blind rage potion rework.

Additions

Glazed Terracotta Pots

  • A new decorative block made from glazed terracotta
  • Can hold items, just like decorated pots
  • Cannot have sherds, unlike decorated pots
  • Very cool and useful for a splash of color in your builds

More Flower Pots

  • You can now make prismarine and nether brick flower pots
  • Made the exact same way as flower pots, just this time with nether bricks or prismarine shards
  • Not much else to them, just useful for decoration

Creaking Connector

  • A brand-new redstone block made from a creaking heart, giving new use to the pale garden
  • Acts as a wireless redstone system
  • When right-clicked with a sapling, it creates a “saplink”, which can be right-clicked to cause the connector to give off a signal
    • The darker the wood of the sapling, the higher the signal that is emitted
      • For example, pale oak gives off a signal of 1, while dark oak gives off 8

End City Loot Improvements

While I was reworking gobblerifts to add gobblerift seeds, I reworked end city loot, replacing the inventory clogging horse armors and saddles with axes, lapis lazuli, and name tags

Changes

Witch Hat

  • You can now dye witch hats like leather armor
  • Have fun color-coordinating your trims with your hat!
  • There’s also a new 2D texture when in the inventory

Blind Rage Potion

Blind rage potions now give strength V instead of strength III

  • This is due to the removal of tea; there is now no item to counteract the blindness, so I felt safe making the amplifier more reasonable
  • This is a reasonable bit more damage than strength II with critical hits, since you cannot crit or sprint while blind

Gobblerift

  • Now found as seeds in end cities AND crashed end ships, rather than just crashed end ships

    • This replaces the beetroot seeds

  • Growing from seeds to fully grown now takes much less bone meal

  • More filling meat (steak, pork) now makes the gobblerift enter digestion faster than less filling meat (chicken, fish)

  • You can now make a gobblerift reproduce by feeding it its favorite food, rabbit stew, for the entirety of a digestion cycle

    • This takes 12 rabbit stew in total, and it will spit out gobblerift seeds when it opens up next

  • You can now break and move gobblerifts without them resetting into the lowest growth stage

    • Anything below the mouth stage of growth drops gobblerift seeds instead (which are stackable, reducing inventory clog)

New Mob Animations

  • All TCC mobs now have new, smoother animations
  • This took eons to do, and relies on the new 1.21.4 composite model system
  • Enchanters and Iceologers also have capes due to this change

Cursed Crown

  • Now takes all but one of your hearts, rather than only 8 of your hearts
  • You can now heal during the cooldown using golden apples
    • Golden apple heals 3 hearts
    • Enchanted golden apple heals 12 hearts
  • The cooldown to fully refresh hearts has been lowered to 8 seconds
  • Now is compatible with health boost and other health-increasing items
  • Now has a 2D texture in the inventory like other helmet items
  • Now prevents piglins from getting angry at you like other gold armor

Crashed End Ship

  • The crashed end ship is no longer the only location where you can find gobblerift seeds
  • It now contains a “tattered elytra”, an elytra with 2 minutes of durability that cannot be repaired or enchanted rather than gobblerifts
    • This acts as a way to break up the tedium of the outer end by giving you a “demo” elytra

Riftjuice Bottle

  • Riftjuice bottles now stack up to 16
  • Now have a unique emissive texture

Silk Touch Improvements

Silk-touched composters and respawn anchors now have lore listing their fill status

Wrench

The wrench can rotate all relevant pale oak and resin blocks

Wandering Trader Rework

The reworked wandering trader now sells pale oak saplings, pale moss, pale hanging moss, and eyeblossoms

Smoldering Seeker

The smoldering seeker’s needle texture now has more consistent shading

Campfire Improvements

Unlit campfires now have static rather than animated textures, for consistency with the other campfire items

Prismarine Pottery

The “voyager” pottery sherd texture has been updated

Ocean Ruin Archaeology Rework

Made sniffer eggs slightly more common in warm ocean ruins

Removals

Positional Anchor

  • This feature allowed you to store ender pearls in a redstone block that would fire them when powered
  • Many of Mojang’s new additions made this block functionally irrelevant, as it is now worse than default ender pearls in bubble columns—it cannot load chunks or work crossdimensionally like stasis chambers can

Tea

  • This feature was awkward and led to several balance issues I was not happy with
  • It allowed you to prevent potion effects from dual potions, and this made blind rage not be able to reach usable potential due to the fear of making it wildly unbalanced due to blindness immunity
  • Allowed you to completely ignore core mechanics of structures, like mining fatigue and darkness
  • Apparently, the majority of you use this feature for the fun of making tea instead of actually using it in gameplay, so I would rather not continue maintaining this feature if the main use is having tea parties

Warped Wart

Removed along with tea. Its only purpose was acting as an ingredient for that and nausea potions (which were unbalanced anyways).

Nausea Potion

Removed due to tea and warped wart being removed.

This potion also had no use in singleplayer as nausea has no effect on mobs.

Snail Mucus

This feature was retextured slime balls that could be used to extend teas

I have simply replaced this with plain old slime, as slime is not exclusive to slime mobs, as baby panda sneezing has proven

Silk Touching Farmland

Removed silk-touching farmland; it was useless and annoying when attempting to dig up farms, just use a hoe again
