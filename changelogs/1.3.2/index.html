1.3.2

January 11, 2024

Yeah, I may have screwed up a tiny bit. Mojang made a tiny change that I missed that happened to fully break both tangling pearls and diluted potions.

So this update has fixes for those, as well a solid bit of new content.

Additions

Phantom Item Frame Sounds

The phantom item frame now has custom sounds, including breaking, placing, adding an item, and removing an item

Elli is a sound wizard!

Fire Boomerangs

You can now catch boomerang projectiles on fire using lava, fire, or campfires

If a boomerang that is on fire breaks a potato, chorus plant, or cactus, the result will be smelted

Boomerangs on fire will also set mobs on fire (not players, since Mojang is stinky)

Pick Blocking

You can now “pick block” positional anchors, soul seers, soul conductors, and pottery tables by control+middle-clicking them

Changes

Witch Hat

The witch hat has been completely reworked to have a new purpose (that isn’t just a cool hat!)

When killing mobs that drop potion ingredients, they will now have higher drop rates of these ingredients. This includes:

  • Gunpowder from creepers
  • Gunpowder/ghast tears from ghasts
  • Magma cream from magma cubes
  • Spider eyes from spiders
  • Sightless eyes from glow lichen zombies
  • Rabbit’s feet from rabbits
  • Phantom membrane from phantoms
  • Amethyst dust from geomancers
  • Random potion ingredients from witches

Wandering Witch

The wandering witch’s trades are now more randomized and have more options

The new trades are as follows:

  • 10 redstone, 10 gunpowder, 10 glowstone dust, 12 glass bottles, or 1 amethyst dust fo* 4 spider eyes
  • Brewing stand or 10 nether wart for 2 fermented spider eyes
  • 4 spider eyes for 4 slime balls
  • 8 spider eyes for a random potion ingredient from the below list:
    • Golden carrot
    • Magma cream
    • Pufferfish
    • Ghast tear
    • Rabbit foot
    • Phantom membrane
    • Sightless eye
    • Heartbeet
  • 8 spider eyes for a different ingredient from the above list
  • 10 spider eyes for mycelium, crimson nylium, or warped nylium
  • 2 fermented spider eyes for a ‘crop’ nether brick pottery sherd
  • 4 fermented spider eyes for an ender pearl
  • 8 fermented spider eyes for a map to an ancient city (66%) or swamp hut (33%)
  • 10 fermented spider eyes for a witch hat

Sightless Eye

Sightless eye drop rates from glow lichen zombies have been increased

Wandering Trader Rework

Wandering traders now sell all variants of snail and axolotl

Axolotl Bucket Variants

The new axolotl bucket textures feature now works in creative mode properly

Snail Nest

Snail nests can now be pushed by pistons

Miscellaneous Changes

  • Lots of commands have been VERY minorly optimized by adding namespaces
  • Slightly optimized raycasts
  • More cat morning gifts has been made into a whole compendium entry rather than being a footnote on the bat wing page
  • Tweaked some compendium entries to have more detail and more images
  • TCC blocks now derender when much further away
  • Translation files have been updated

Fixes

  • #187: Tangling Pearl is unable to toggle into enabled mode

  • #188: Diluted potion brewing leads to uncraftable potions with no effects

  • #189: TCC potted plants can be placed multiple at a time

  • #190: Incorrect block on ocean monument

  • #191: Enchanters’ effects are no longer ambient (translucent particles)

  • #192: Snails are unbucketed off-center
