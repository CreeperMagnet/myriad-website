0.5.0: Caves and Waves

June 10, 2021

This update has been quite the experience… not only have we expanded our development horizons, but we’ve also sped up our development cycle tenfold. Nine betas over the course of four months, and tons and tons of content.

Well, let’s get into that changelog already, since that’s why you’re here!

It’s a pretty massive one this time, including tons of features for both caves (The geomancer, amethyst dust, glow berry muffins, etc.) and waves (a full ocean monument rework, snails, coral drowned, etc.). The 1.17 update gave us tons of ideas, and as you can see, tons of them have been implemented.

Additions

Snail

Spawn in swamps

Can be bucketed

When fed beetroot, they will spawn snail mucus items for 20 minutes (one in-game day)

  • Feeding them beetroot also gives them a short regeneration effect

Take damage in salty or dry areas

  • Salty areas means blocks filled with water in ocean biomes, since those would naturally have salt
  • Dry areas include deserts, mesas, savannas and the nether

Snail Mucus

A slime equivalent, spawned by snails that have been fed beetroot

  • Can be used in recipes anywhere a normal slime ball can be

You can also put snail mucus into your tea to increase the duration of the negative effect immunity from 5:00 to 7:30

Phantom Item Frame

Created by making an item frame with a phantom membrane instead of leather

Turns invisible when there’s an item inside it

Dyed Bundles

Pretty self-explanatory

Made by placing dye in the middle of the bundle recipe

Re-added the bundle recipe along with this feature

Cannot re-dye an existing bundle

Ocean Monument Rework

As you saw in the thumbnail for this update, there’s a whole new ocean monument!

Includes a trident and improved loot along with an improved build

Large Ocean Ruin Additions

The large variants of ocean ruins will now spawn with a small addition with extra loot

This addition can either have 2 nautilus shells and some loot or a trident

Coral Drowned

Drowned can spawn with coral on them

These variants will drop the block that is on them, allowing early-game access to these plants

Sponge Drowned

Drowned can spawn with sponge on them

This makes sponge renewable

Photometer

Used to measure light level

Has four stages

  • Lowest light, light levels 0-3
  • Low light, light levels 4-7
  • High light, light levels 8-11
  • Highest light, light levels 12-15

Each of these four stages corresponds to a certain kind of mob spawning, so you don’t need to worry about specific numbers, and have a simplistic GUI instead

At lowest light, bats will spawn, at low light, normal overworld monsters spawn, at high light, monsters will spawn in the nether, and at highest light, nothing spawns

Villager Bed Exploding

Villagers that try to use a bed in the nether or end will now explode. If you can’t do it, neither can they!

Ring Additions

Netherite Rings

  • A new ring with 175 durability and fire immunity
  • Has the potion properties of gold rings

Amethyst Gemstone

  • Made by putting amethyst shards in a stonecutter
  • Applies the “Item Preservation” custom attribute when on a ring in your offhand
    • This attribute increases the time it takes for your items to despawn
      • It increases the timer from 5 minutes to 10 normally, and 5 to 15 when pristine
    • It also causes items you drop to glow
    • All of the above behavior applies to items dropped when you die
  • When you die with multiple items, the durability taken is equal to the items it causes to glow
  • Dropping an item normally takes one durability from the ring

Charged Creeper Sounds

Charged creepers now have ambient sounds

Wither Skeletons With Bows

Wither skeletons now have a 1% chance to spawn with a bow instead of a sword

Glow Lichen Zombie

Zombies covered in glow lichen can spawn below y=0

They give blindness for 30s on hit

They drop lots of glow lichen

Glow Berry Muffin

A simple variant of the Sweet Berry Muffin

Crafted with glow berries instead of sweet berries

Gives same amount of hunger as the usual sweet berry muffin

Geomancer

Replace all witches below y=40

Attack using different kinds of pillars, which can either entrap you in deepslate or explode you

Drops amethyst dust, deepslate, amethyst shards, and rarely a pristine amethyst gemstone

Comes with a spawn egg that is obtainable via peculiar berries

Amethyst Dust

The new fuel for jewelry tables (see the jewelry table rework under changes)

Dropped by not fully grown amethyst buds (NOT budding amethyst blocks) and geomancers

Boomerang Vehicle Breaking

Boomerangs can now break minecarts and boats when they hit them

  • This does not apply to spawner minecarts or command block minecarts to prevent survival mode players from obtaining or breaking creative mode items

This feature was suggested by Skaliber. Thanks, Skal!

New Villager Ring Trades

Added new trades to the mason and toolsmith

  • The mason will now give you a random gemstone in exchange for emeralds
  • The mason will also give you emeralds for raw gemstone ingredients (prismarine crystals, obsidian shards, amethyst shards)
  • The toolsmith now trades enchanted golden/iron rings
  • The toolsmith will also buy 4-5 amethyst dust for 1 emerald

Removed their “cycling” trades, since they were unintuitive and inconsistent with vanilla gameplay

New Sounds

New paintbrush and teapot sounds

Consistent Trapdoor Recipes

All trapdoor recipes now give 6 trapdoors

Miscellaneous Additions

Recipe Output Texture

  • The default “click to craft” knowledge book has now been renamed to “Recipe Output” and has a special texture
  • Looks very good!

New Advancements

  • Several new advancements for V0.5 content have been added, such as “Geode Geezer” for killing a geomancer, “Snail Snatcher” for capturing a snail in a bucket, and many more

Changes

Jewelry Table

Now requires amethyst dust for fuel (Is used up when adding gemstones, potions, etc.)

Pristine gemstones are no longer craftable through random chance when stonecutting crystalline items

Adding 64 amethyst dust creates a pristine ring

The enchantment slot has been removed, along with the ability to add the Flame, Channeling, and Thorns enchantments. These enchantments were stealing the spotlight from vanilla items, couldn’t be applied via anvil, and didn’t take XP. Now, all the valid enchantments (Vanishing, Mending, Unbreaking), can be applied via anvil, rather than the jewelry table.

The jewelry table may be a tad laggy in this snapshot, due to a Mojang bug that should be fixed by next beta.

The jewelry table recipe has been updated to include an amethyst shard, so you can’t make it until you have the proper fuel to use it. You can find this recipe in the compendium.

A small “recipe book” has been added to the side to give you some hints on how to properly craft rings

Obsidian Mirror Rework

Now flips positive effects along with negative effects

Limits the maximum flipped effect amplifier to II. This means if you flip poison IV, you will only get regeneration II.

No longer takes damage when used in creative mode

Cursed Crown

Now comes with built-in curse of binding

Has 100 durability

  • It takes damage when you take damage, or when you kill a mob

Now accepts helmet enchantments

Can no longer turn farming loot to gold

Can no longer turn already golden items into gold nuggets

Ancient Acorn

Heavily reduced duration from two hours down to five minutes

Are now traded by wandering traders at a price of 20-30 emeralds

Paintbrush

Paintbrushes can now paint candles

Ring Changes

The emerald ring now works on axolotls, goats, and glow squids

Peculiar Berries

Chickens fed peculiar berries will now lay axolotl, goat, husk, stray, and glow squid spawn eggs if they are in the proper biome

Anvil Smashing

Anvils can now smash amethyst and tinted glasss

Block Breakers

Block breakers can now be crafted with blackstone or deepslate

Tea

Clearsight tea is now made using glow lichen, rather than glowstone dust

Silk Touch Infested Deepslate

Infested deepslate can be silk-touched, just like all other infested blocks

Frostbloom

Frostblooms now fill cauldrons with powdered snow

Increased Shulker Drops

Shulkers now drop two shells 100% of the time with looting III

Locket of Learning

The locket of learning now spawns an experience orb rather than using /xp, which means you can use it to store experience to repair your tools

New Textures

Tons and tons of texture changes. Dyed bundles, frostblooms, frostbloom petals, gobblerifts, sweet berry muffins, swordfish, jewelry tables, enchanters, iceologers, cakes, bat wings, lockets of learning, peculiar berries, rings, smoldering seekers, teacups, and rotten porkchops. Basically anything that looked kinda iffy last version. They’ve all been fixed up. Frostblooms are my particular favorite this version, they’ve definitely got quite the glow-up. Especially the petals, too!

Tundra Keep

Added frost walker books to the tundra keep loot table

Compendium

Changed the credits of the compendium around a bit

Made the compendium always use the default Minecraft font so none of its features are broken when using another resource pack

Illager Changes

Made it so viewing illager entities in spectator mode looks somewhat proper, rather than a broken z-fighting mess

Iceologer and geomancer attacks now benefit from the strength effect

Anvil Smashing

Tweaked the effects for smashing glowstone with an anvil

Miscellaneous Changes

1.17 updates:

  • Updated nearly everything to 1.17
  • Many pieces of code have been updated to use 1.17’s new item modifier mechanic, making things a lot cleaner and efficient
    • This does not include rings due to the ongoing jewelry table rework
  • Relevant changes are noted in above sections
  • Updated the majority of code so it no longer uses a shulker box in order to load the proper items for item modification
  • Updated the block breakers to follow the new added tool block tags
    • This comes with new “additions” and “denied” block tags for each block breaker. If you want to allow a specific block breaker to mine a block, simply add it to the “additions” list. The “denied” list is the exact opposite, preventing the block breaker from mining said block.
  • Updated all custom blocks that used to use the “Fire” tag to now use “HasVisualFire” for rendering

Removed a bunch of clutter in the ring files

Reformatted the pack.mcmetas

Riftjuice dimensions now uses function tags

  • This means if your pack has a custom dimension, you can make it work with TCC by adding two functions and two function tags to your pack!

Optimized some selectors, removing sort=arbitrary since that’s the default

Removals

Instant Effect Tipped Swords

Removed the ability to add instant potion effects such as instant damage and health to swords

Drowned Loot Changes

Drowned dropping prismarine crystals and sponge. This has been replaced with the drowned variants feature.

Drowned holding tridents having a 25% chance to drop them. This abundance has been lowered back to normal levels, now that every monument has a trident and ocean ruins rarely have tridents.

Silk-Touching Cauldrons

The ability to silk-touch cauldrons has been removed since Mojang removed the blockstate behind the cauldron item.

Fixes

Fixed a longtime issue with recipe packs giving all of TCC’s items when they unlocked recipes; now, this only applies to gemstones.

Removed a deprecated library from the code

Removed some unused compatibility textures for rings

You can now pick up more things from custom TCC blocks when your inventory is full, including tea from teapots and riftjuice from gobblerifts

Invisible display models can no longer be held in your inventory, even if you somehow manage to get them
