1.1.0 Beta 1

June 20, 2022

This is a work-in-progress beta version. There may be incomplete features or bugs, and everything is subject to change. Make backups, proceed at your own risk, and have fun!

It’s been a long time since the last update… wait. It’s only been three months? Seriously? Well, I guess I’m getting better at putting these updates out faster.

However, this is also only a beta for the 1.1 update, which I will keep unnamed since there are still features to release.

Even if it’s a beta, that doesn’t mean it doesn’t have lots of features! Let’s get into the changes!

Additions

Mangrove Swamp Village

A new village variant that spawns in mangrove swamps

Has unique mangrove buildings

Spawns the previously unused swamp villager variant

Has an explorer map traded by cartographers 50% of the time for 20-30 emeralds. The other 50% of the time, cartographers will trade maps to the tundra keep.

Geode Outpost

A new structure, found by following a “Geode Explorer Map” traded by jeweler villagers

Contains at least four geomancers inside

The loot includes enough material to make a single ring, one budding amethyst, and a silk touch iron pickaxe

Once you clear out the geomancers, they don’t respawn, so the massive geode can be harvested safely

Soul Conductor

A new block crafted from 2 echo shards

Connecting two soul conductors with a line of sculk and clicking one end will teleport you to the other

You can find out more about this feature in its guide article. Yes, it’s so complex it has its own guide article.

Disc 5 Renewability

Creepers killed by the warden will now drop disc 5 fragments

Geomancer

Echo shards now drop from geomancers killed nearby a sculk catalyst, instead of their usual amethyst shard drop, making echo shards renewable

Geomancers drop budding amethyst 2.5% of the time

Illumination Tea

You can now brew tea using an echo shard to create Tea of Illumination, which gives darkness immunity

Elder Guardian Improvements

To go with the new ocean monument rework (described below), the elder guardians now give the mining fatigue effect much faster, and will clear the effect once you kill all three

Wrench

Wrenches can now rotate creative mode blocks when you’re in creative mode and new 1.19 blocks

Changes

Ocean Monument Rework (Rework)

The ocean monument rework has been completely overhauled to have multiple rooms and a better palette. (no more glass!)

It no longer uses barrels for loot, and instead has all loot in block form. It rewards:

  • 24 gold blocks
  • 32 sponge blocks
  • One trident, now encased in a prismarine cage

Soul Seer

The soul seer is now crafted with deepslate and a single echo shard, rather than soul sand

It now triggers observers when activated and deactivated

It also has an amazing new texture, shown above

Positional Anchor

The positional anchor is now crafted with blackstone, rather than deepslate, to fit the nether theme more clearly

The pearl now comes out 0.1 blocks from the top of the block, rather than 0.5 blocks from the top

It also has an amazing new texture, shown above

Jeweler

The jeweler now trades calcite and a map to a geode outpost along with its previous trades

Golden Apple Pie

Golden apple pie slices now give the same duration of effect for each slice

Tea

Mining fatigue immunity tea is now made from sponge water

  • Made by clicking a wet sponge on a teapot, which will give you a dry sponge in return
  • Sponge drowned only spawn in monuments due to this change in order to prevent monument cheesing

Witch Trading

Witches now trade sightless eyes for their final trade instead of ancient acorns

Rings

Diamond rings now give +2 and +4 hearts, rather than +1 and +2

  • Hopefully this brings them more in line with the other rings!

Boomerang

Boomerangs can now break cobwebs, fully grown mangrove propagules, and chest boats

Wandering Trader Rework

Wandering traders no longer trade snail mucus, they now only trade snail buckets

Removals

Large Ocean Ruin Additions

This feature was removed simply because it didn’t add much that the monument rework didn’t.

It also had a small chance of breaking due to the postgen method it used.

Absorption Stacking

Very overpowered.

You could eat multiple golden apples to stack the absorption hearts, which could effectively allow you to get a full row of absorption and cheese large damage very easily.

Geomancer Deepslate Drop

Geomancers no longer drop deepslate

Ancient Acorn

Also very overpowered.

You could eat an ancient acorn to get five minutes of haste II. They were traded by wandering traders and dropped by spruce leaves in mega taigas—meaning you could easily obtain them and not ever need a beacon.

They could also be used to brew mining fatigue immunity tea—that role has been given to wet sponges instead.

Fixes

  • #111: Snail compendium entry has misleading info about hatching time

  • #113: Witch Hats have RepairCost of 100, preventing enchanting and renaming via anvil

  • #114: Cartographer zombies are improperly converted into jeweler zombies if they have unlocked the tundra keep trade

  • #115: Molten mirror does not flip luck into bad luck

  • #116: Diamond rings take extra damage from invalid sources, while prismarine and quartz rings take no damage at all

  • #117: Ocean monument reworks sometimes do not spawn in specific circumstances

  • #118: Soul Seers cannot be crafted more than once

  • #119: Illagers do not despawn in peaceful mode

  • #121: Iceologers do not spawn in raids on frozen ocean biomes
