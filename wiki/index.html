Accessible Music Discs

Several music discs are now more accessible or made renewable.

Amethyst Dust

The powdered version of amethyst shards, used as an ingredient.

Ancient City Archaeology

Ancient cities now have several archaeology sites filled with loot and secrets.

Ancient City Loot Rework

Ancient cities now have improved loot with less clogging unstackable items.

Apple Pie

A tasty new food item, and the apple equivalent to pumpkin pie.

Archaeologist

A new villager type that trades archaeology-related items.

Armor Stands with Arms

The same armor stand you know, now with arms and poses. Just add two sticks!

Armor Trim Addon

The installation guide for using the armor trim addon, as well as a guide to finding all the new armor trims included.

Axolotl Bucket Variants

Axolotl buckets now display their variants, instead of having a pink axolotl for all textures. Babies are also displayed!

Badlands Temple

The badlands temple is a dangerous structure with traps and loot galore.

Banner Pattern Duplication

You can now duplicate banner patterns by combining them with a piece of paper.

Bat Wing

A new ingredient dropped by bat wings that can be crafted into leather.

Better Mangrove Roots

Mangrove roots are now less of a hassle to deal with, only dropping themselves when mined with silk touch or shears.

Biome Based Fishing

Rarities of fish are now different depending on which biome you are fishing in.

Boomerang

A fun weapon and even more fun farming tool. Harvest & replant crops, kill your enemies, or just use it as a fidget toy.

Brush Painting

Brushes can now be used with dye in the other hand to change the color of blocks using the brush’s durability.

Cake Improvements

Cakes can now be mined and moved without silk touch.

Campfire Improvements

Campfires now have an unlit variant that drops when mining them with an axe without silk touch.

Cat Improvements

Cats now have more loot in their morning gift pool, and can be tamed using tropical fish.

Cherry Grove Village

A cherry grove variant of villages. Very pretty, very pink.

Cinnabar Recipe

Cinnabar is now craftable using netherrack and sulfur spikes.

Climbable Chains

Chains can now be climbed like ladders.

Compass Cloning Recipe

Lodesotne compasses can be cloned by putting two in the crafting grid.

Consistent Dye Recipes

Several dye recipes are now more consistent.

Consistent Loom Recipe

The loom recipe is now more consistent with other workstations’ recipes.

Consistent Rib Armor Trim Duplication

Rib armor trim templates are now duplicated with nether bricks instead of netherrack.

Consistent Stair Recipe Yield

All stair recipes now give an output of 6 instead of 4.

Consistent Trapdoor Recipes

Trapdoor recipes now have sensible output amounts, and metal trapdoor recipes are improved.

Coral Drowned

A new cosmetic variant of drowned that drops coral, making it more accessible early-game.

Coral Recipes

Coral blocks are now craftable from coral and coral fans in a 2x2.

Cracked Deepslate Tile Recipes

Cracked deepslate tiles can now be crafted from cracked deepslate bricks.

Craftable Mushroom Blocks

Mushroom blocks are now craftable using 4 mushrooms.

Craftable Nautilus Armor

Nautilus armor is now craftable using 8 of an armor material and a nautilus shell.

Craftable Snout Banner Pattern

The snout banner pattern can now be crafted with a piglin head and a piece of paper.

Crashed End Ship

A spooky, abandoned end structure found rarely in the outer end islands.

Creaking Connector

A new redstone component that allows transfer of signals over long distances.

Creaking Crypt

A new pale garden structure filled with archaeology, loot, and plenty of creaking.

Crescent Banner Pattern

A moon-shaped banner pattern found in the creaking crypt.

Cursed Crown

A unique armor item that replaces your health with regenerating absorption hearts.

Dead Bush Recipes

Dead bushes can now be crafted using both types of bushes in furnaces.

Desert Well Rework

The desert well now has slightly better archaeology loot and two layers of suspicious sand.

Diluted Potions

A potion type you can take your time sipping. One full duration of potion spread over multiple uses.

Dragon Head Renewability

The ender dragon now drops its head when killed by a charged creeper.

Dry Grass Beetroot Seeds

Dry grass now has a chance to drop beetroot seeds.

Dyed Bundles

Bundles can now be dyed all 16 colors. Great for organization!

Dyed Compasses

Compasses can now be dyed all 16 colors. Perfect for keeping track of multiple different lodestones.

Dyed Written Books

Written books can now be dyed all 16 colors.

Echo Shard Renewability

Echo shards are now renewable using a dangerous interaction between sculk shriekers and amethyst clusters.

Echoing Hourglass

A magical block that allows its user to sift through the sands of time to renew archaeology loot.

Elder Guardian Improvements

Elder guardians have been slightly improved to have more consistent mining fatigue behavior. Guardians struck by lightning also turn into elder guardians.

Emoji

A way to express all your villager-based emotions in chat with a handy menu that lets you choose which emoji to send.

Enchanted Golden Carrot

The rare loot-exclusive variant of the golden carrot that gives night vision for 40 minutes.

Enchanter

The support class of illagers that call the woodland mansion rework their home.

End City Loot Improvements

End city loot has been reworked to be slightly more tolerable.

Fishing Treasure Particles

Bubble popping particles now show up in areas where fishing will yield no treasure.

Frost Walker Mounts

When riding a mount while wearing frost walker boots, they leave a trail of frosted ice.

Frost Walker Speed

When walking on ice while wearing frost walker, you receive a speed boost.

Frostbloom

A unique flower that creates an aura of snow around it.

Frostbloom Petals

A frosty floral treat that grant regeneration at the cost of mobility.

Fungal Stew

A nether equivalent to mushroom stew, crafted with warped and crimson fungi.

Geomancer

A crotchety old illager that lives deep down in the deepslate layer of the world. Watch out for his explosive pillars.

Gilded Blackstone Rework

Gilded blackstone now functions more like an ore.

Glazed Terracotta Pots

A handy set of decoration blocks made from glazed terracotta. Store your items in style!

Glow Berry Muffin

A sweet treat made from glow berries. More filling than normal glow berries and quite a bit more convenient.

Glow Lichen Zombie

A blind, lichen-covered zombie variant found in the deepslate layer of the world.

Gobblerift

A strange plant native to the End. Drinking its riftjuice allows for long-distance teleportation.

Golden Apple Pie

A new pastry variation on golden apples, converting their healing effect into haste and speed instead.

Golden Chorus Fruit

Chorus fruit, but much much more effective. Throw yourself to the far reaches of your world with a single bite.

Grouped Glazed Terracotta Recipes

Glazed terracotta recipes are now grouped in the furnace recipe book.

Hay Vibration Dampening

Hay blocks can now be used to dampen sculk sensor vibrations in addition to wool.

Heartbeet

A healing variant of beetroot that rarely drops from beetroot crops. They can be used in health boost potions.

Hoglin Improvements

Hoglins now have 2-3 babies when breeding instead of just one.

Horse Armor Improvements

Horse armor is now craftable and enchantable. It has also been removed from many loot tables to reduce inventory clutter.

Iceologer

A dangerous ice wizard that summons blocks of falling ice. Can be found chilling out in tundra keeps.

Improved Prismarine Recipes

Prismarine recipes are now more consistent with other recipes in the game.

Improved Trident Enchantments

The impaling and loyalty trident enchantments have several improvements to make them more usable.

Increased Chain Yield

Chain recipes now give 4 chains instead of 1.

Increased Emerald Ore Drops

Emerald ore now drops 1-4 emeralds to make emerald ore more useful when compared to getting all your emeralds from villagers.

Increased Lodestone Yield

The Lodestone recipe now yields eight lodestones instead of just one. Makes the netherite ingot investment more worthwhile.

Increased Shulker Drops

Shulkers now drop 1-2 shulker shells instead of 0-1. With looting III, they will always drop two shells.

Increased TNT Yield

The TNT recipe now yields four TNT instead of just one. Makes it more viable to craft TNT instead of using TNT dupers.

Jungle Temple Rework

The jungle temple has been reworked to include better traps, loot, and puzzles. It also has an archaeology section and exclusive sherds as well.

Learning Enchantment

A sword & spear enchantment that increases experience drops from mobs, at the cost of being incompatible with looting.

Locket of Learning

A helpful enchanting tool that allows you to store up to thirty levels of experience points. It is also the main reward of the reworked woodland mansion.

Mace Renewability

Maces are now renewable via the rare chance of zombies and husks spawning holding them during ominous trials.

Mangrove Swamp Village

A new village variant found in the mangrove swamp. A rare find, hidden deep between mangrove roots.

More Copper Smeltables

More items can be smelted down into copper nuggets, including spyglasses, brushes, and trial keys.

More Flower Pots

Flower pots can now be made using prismarine sherds, resin bricks, or nether bricks.

More Illager Banners

More illager banners (similar to the ominous banner) can be found in all of Myriad’s new illager structures.

More Mason Trades

Masons can now trade tuff, deepslate, and calcite.

More Mineable Blocks

More blocks now have proper tools that allow them to be mined faster.

More Mossy Recipes

Mossy blocks can now be crafted more efficiently using a single moss block and the block you want to mossify.

More Potted Plants

More plants can now be placed in flower pots, including two-block-tall flowers, different kinds of grass, and unique Myriad plants.

More Sniffer Loot

Sniffers now dig up more ancient seeds at a time, and can also dig up frostblooms.

More Stone Substitutions

Deepslate and blackstone can now be used as substitutes for stone in more recipes.

More Suspicious Stews

Suspicious stews can now be made with more flowers, including chorus flowers, flowering azaleas, cactus flowers, pink petals, and wildflowers.

More Undead Nugget Drops

Zombies, husks, and drowned now drop nuggets similarly to zombified piglins. A cheap, consistent income of metal and a better drop than rotten flesh.

Nether Reactor

A feature resurrected from ancient versions of pocket edition, but given a whole new purpose. Spend a nether star on a nether reactor core and build its casing to create an area of fire resistance.

Ocean Monument Rework

The ocean monument has been reworked to bring it up to modern structure standards. More loot, more depth, and a better build.

Ocean ruins now have better archaeology, including a mysterious new mob egg and several new prismarine sherds.

A new mossy skeleton variant that spawns in jungles, ambushing unsuspecting players with poison arrows.

Paintbrush

A building tool used to paint blocks using dye.

Phantom Item Frame

Pig Improvements

Pigs now have 2-3 babies at once instead of just one, making them more effective competitors against cows or sheep.

Piglin Bartering Archaeology

Piglins can now rarely barter suspicious gravel with loot inside instead of normal gravel.

A useful redstone block that keeps an ender pearl in stasis for later teleportation.

Potion of Blind Rage

A risky potion that allows you to deal a massive amount of damage with the drawback of being fully blind.

Potion of Fortitude

A health boost potion, brewed from heartbeets.

Pottery Table

A block used to decoration pots and duplicate sherds. It’s also the workstation for the new archaeologist villager.

Prismarine Pottery

An ocean-based style of pottery made from prismarine, including 10 sherd patterns found in ocean ruins.

Quarry Remnant

A long-forgotten mine found deep underneath the basalt deltas.

Ravagers now drop the sentry armor trim rarely, instead of just a saddle.

A unique tracking device made from netherite that points to a mysterious nether structure…

Recovery Compass Rework

Recovery compasses are now cheaper and make the items from your last death glow while held in your hand.

Refuge Remains

The nether counterpart to the trail ruins, including plenty of loot and nether brick pottery sherds.

Resin Brick Pottery

A resin-based style of pottery, including 8 new sherd patterns found in various structures.

Resin Candy

A sweet treat made from resin clumps and sugar. It awakens at night, becoming more filling when eaten.

Riftjuice Bottle

The liquid product of the gobblerift, used to teleport back to the gobblerift it was harvested from.

Warped, crimson, and hanging roots can all be smelted down into dyes.

Rooted Dirt Recipe

Rooted dirt can now be crafted using dirt and hanging roots.

Sculk veins can now be crafted into sculk blocks.

Sightless Eye

A rare potion ingredient dropped by glow lichen zombies and used to brew potions of blind rage.

Silk Touch Improvements

Silk touch now picks up grass paths, filled composters, infested blocks, and charged respawn anchors.

Smoldering Seeker

A new compass variant made from nether bricks that points to the nearest nether fortress.

Snail Mucus
