Woodland Mansion Rework

The woodland mansion has been completely reworked, to include lots of new rooms, better loot, and enchanters. the main reward for the mansion, the locket of learning, is located in the hidden basement section. Keep an eye out for hidden rooms!

The basement also has hidden archaeology rooms with the “Moon”, “Shade”, and “Slumber” resin brick sherds, as well as a map to the creaking crypt. This is also the only place to find the “Vex” armor trim. Look for cracks in the walls!

The entrance to the mansion. That bookshelf on the top left side looks odd…

Hidden rooms have hints such as red candles, cracked walls, and even special wall patterns…

History

1.6.0Release🞵

The mansion has now been brought more in line with its vanilla counterpart

  • It has less overuse of stairs overall
  • Some of the missing rooms from vanilla have been brought back, such as:
    • The illager statue room
    • The tulip shrine room
    • The chicken statue room
    • And much more!
  • Brought back the exterior stone trim, but as a more elegant stone brick trim instead of cobblestone

Added lots of new rooms

Reworked the basement archaeology rooms:

  • Now contain 3 resin pottery sherds instead of 2 brick sherds. These brick sherds have been moved to the ancient city.
  • Depict creaking/pale garden content instead of other unrelated bits
  • Has one guaranteed hidden room that contains a map to a creaking crypt

There are also plenty of hidden rooms accessible via chiseled bookshelf

Beta 2🞵
  • Updated mushroom farm to use podzol so mushrooms no longer break in light
  • Added a hidden chest to a large storage room in the basement
  • Ensured consistent railing design for mansion basement rooms
  • Added two new banner/loom rooms
Beta 1🞵
  • The mansion has been completely reworked once again
  • The gameplay is mostly the same, but much of the build has been updated
  • This includes:
    • Consistent bookshelf, chandelier, shelf, and table style
    • More room variations & removal of boring rooms
    • Bringing back better versions of missing vanilla rooms
    • An overhaul of the exterior to be more in line with vanilla
  • There are also 3 new hidden rooms accessible via chiseled bookshelf
  • Archaeology is now guaranteed in the basement section, and it includes a map to the creaking crypt
